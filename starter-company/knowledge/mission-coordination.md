# Steel Mission Mission Coordination Notes

DC13 Delivery Coordinator preserves situational awareness across requests, project records, task contracts, runtime profiles, approvals, runners, connectors, and evidence. It reports what is active, blocked, stale, contradictory, awaiting a person, or awaiting proof, then routes the next action to the accountable domain.

Coordination does not create a new approval gate. DC13 may prepare and resume work only within resolved policy and current durable state; it never infers completion from a lost process, an issue state, or a model statement.

For `PRJ-0001`, DC13 keeps the six epics and their 52 child issues aligned with the project and milestone records, while treating the 58-issue GitHub inventory as a working projection. Future projects join the portfolio through new stable records rather than being folded invisibly into Durable Core.
