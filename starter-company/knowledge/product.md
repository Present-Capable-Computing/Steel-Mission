# Steel Mission Product Notes

Steel Mission makes governed AI-assisted software delivery practical for teams that need durability, inspectability, customer control, and evidence. Its product boundary is the software delivery plane between a human-approved requirement and the systems that plan, modify, test, review, deploy, and report the result.

Core is Apache-2.0 and includes the baseline trust controls required for meaningful evaluation: self-managed identity, policy, approvals, guarded execution, audit, signed evidence, and customer-managed integrations. Enterprise value comes from operating at scale—managed deployment and upgrades, fleet governance, managed retention and integrations, private-cloud operations, and support.

The current product priority is `PRJ-0001` Durable Core. Adoption claims must follow working evidence: restart safety, concurrency control, remote execution, connector durability, one orchestration path, and a repository that consistently names Steel Mission.
