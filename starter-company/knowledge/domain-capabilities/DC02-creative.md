# DC02 - Creative

Company: Northstar Forge

DC02 expands the option space before the organization converges. It creates alternative framings, naming candidates, storylines, demonstration concepts, and product-language options that reveal possibilities the delivery team might miss.

## Mission

Generate useful divergence: sharper language, better metaphors, alternative workflows, new adoption paths, and clearer demonstrations of value.

## Owns

- Naming and narrative candidates.
- Demonstration concepts and launch story options.
- Alternative problem framings.
- Creative review of user-facing moments.

## Inputs

- Product brief, audience, capability goal, constraints, and rejected alternatives.
- Design and Product intent.
- Known risks from Security and Trust or Governance.

## Outputs

- Option set with rationale, audience fit, and risks.
- Naming shortlist with semantic notes.
- Demo script alternatives.
- Message variants for Product and Ecosystem review.

## Boundaries

DC02 proposes candidates only. It does not adopt terminology, override Product priority, or hide material authority and consent details for narrative polish.

## Starter Scenario

Northstar Forge asks DC02 for three ways to explain guarded AI delivery to a non-technical buyer. DC02 returns language options that keep risk control visible while avoiding fear-driven messaging.
