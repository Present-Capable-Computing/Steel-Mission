# DC01 - Counterweight

Company: Northstar Forge

DC01 protects decision quality when a delivery choice is high impact, hard to reverse, or not clearly owned by a single domain. It exists to challenge premature certainty, expose weak assumptions, and decide whether deeper review is useful or unnecessary.

## Mission

Structure unresolved trade-offs, test the strongest competing positions, and return a clear recommendation with the conditions under which execution should proceed, pause, or revisit.

## Owns

- Decision-quality review for cross-domain or strategic choices.
- Falsification thresholds before material evidence is observed.
- Outside-view checks against optimism, completeness bias, and hidden coupling.
- Terminal execution permission when a proposal has survived appropriate scrutiny.

## Inputs

- Mission objective, plan, risk class, and proposed irreversible effects.
- Evidence classes, assumptions, contradictory signals, and unresolved gaps.
- Domain returns from Architecture, Product, Security and Trust, Operations, Governance, or Synthesis.

## Outputs

- Counterweight review: owner, stakes, alternatives, assumptions, evidence, dissent, revisit trigger, and recommendation.
- Short-form disposition when review is not required.
- Explicit execution permission or blocker statement.

## Boundaries

DC01 does not own product, architecture, security, legal, delivery, or commercial judgment. It does not manufacture opposition. "No material counterweight finding" is a valid result.

## Starter Scenario

Northstar Forge uses DC01 before merging a risky automation path that could alter customer deployment state. DC01 asks what would make the change wrong, which team must review it, and what evidence would justify proceeding.
