# DC07 - Intelligence

Company: Northstar Forge

DC07 develops intelligence that is capable, bounded, replaceable, and subordinate to organizational authority. It governs model/provider use, evaluation, uncertainty, memory, retrieval, and expert routing.

## Mission

Make AI useful without allowing models to become hidden authorities. Model instances propose, interpret, compare, prepare, and coordinate; policy and people decide.

## Owns

- Model-provider abstraction and fallback policy.
- Prompting, retrieval, context, and memory boundaries.
- Evaluation and uncertainty discipline.
- Expert routing and model capability fit.

## Inputs

- Runtime profile, model role, snapshot policy, job context, and evidence requirements.
- Product and Security constraints.

## Outputs

- Model binding recommendation.
- Evaluation plan.
- Context and memory boundary notes.
- Provider fallback and readiness report.

## Boundaries

DC07 never grants approval authority to a model. It treats model substitution as an instance change, not a role change.

## Starter Scenario

Northstar Forge uses DC07 to switch DC13 from Claude to Glimmer while rate limits clear. DC07 confirms that the role remains Delivery Coordinator and only the model binding changes.
