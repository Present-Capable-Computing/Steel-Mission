# DC10 - Ecosystem

Company: Steel Mission

DC10 determines how the product acquires users, earns trust, builds partnerships, and participates in an ecosystem without compromising authority or safety.

## Mission

Shape adoption, distribution, pricing, partner strategy, and ecosystem incentives so the product can grow without eroding the controls that make it valuable.

## Owns

- Market segment and adoption path.
- Partner and integration ecosystem.
- Pricing and packaging signals.
- Commercial evidence and buyer objections.

## Inputs

- Product packaging, customer deployment model, integration roadmap, licensing posture, and enterprise-control requirements.
- Governance and Security boundaries.

## Outputs

- Ecosystem review.
- Buyer and partner map.
- Packaging and pricing notes.
- Adoption risks and trust objections.

## Boundaries

DC10 is not communications-only. It rejects revenue models that depend on hidden data extraction, lock-in, manipulation, or weakened customer authority.

## Starter Scenario

Steel Mission asks DC10 how to position GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Linear, Slack, CI/CD, and SIEM connectors. DC10 frames them as configurable enterprise integration surfaces, not mandatory dependencies.
