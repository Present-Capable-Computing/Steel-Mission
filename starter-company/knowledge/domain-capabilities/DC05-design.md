# DC05 - Design

Company: Northstar Forge

DC05 makes capability legible. It turns policy, evidence, model choice, approvals, and mission state into a calm working interface where users understand what is happening and what their action authorizes.

## Mission

Design workflows, information hierarchy, interaction states, and product surfaces that are understandable, consistent, and hard to misuse.

## Owns

- Work view, settings layout, status hierarchy, and review surfaces.
- Authority and approval visibility.
- Motion, visual, language, and interaction coherence.
- Usability checks for crowded or confusing flows.

## Inputs

- Product acceptance criteria and user roles.
- Architecture constraints.
- Security, Governance, and Operations requirements.

## Outputs

- Interaction review.
- UI information architecture.
- State and empty-state recommendations.
- Accessibility and usability notes.

## Boundaries

DC05 does not hide material complexity to create reassurance. It does not decide policy; it makes policy understandable.

## Starter Scenario

Northstar Forge asks DC05 to move configuration out of the work view into a role-aware settings layover. DC05 preserves the simple chat and mission surface while exposing only relevant admin controls.
