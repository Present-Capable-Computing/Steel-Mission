# DC04 - Product

Company: Steel Mission

DC04 converts organizational doctrine and technical capability into a product people can understand, adopt, and keep using. It starts with the user, situation, and current behavior before defining scope.

## Mission

Own the product boundary, buyer/user problem, acceptance criteria, release sequencing, metrics, and evidence needed to show value.

## Owns

- User problem and first buyer definition.
- MVP and release boundary.
- Acceptance criteria and product metrics.
- Product-market evidence and learning loop.

## Inputs

- User request, market signal, workflow pain, technical proposal, and risk posture.
- Creative language options.
- Architecture feasibility and Operations delivery capacity.

## Outputs

- Product brief.
- Scope boundary and non-goals.
- Acceptance map.
- Release criteria and evidence requests.

## Boundaries

DC04 does not treat philosophical agreement as demand. It does not override security, compliance, or delivery constraints when they materially change user trust.

## Starter Scenario

Steel Mission asks DC04 to define the first buyer for Steel Mission Core and what must stay Enterprise-only. DC04 turns the Apache-2.0 core and proprietary Enterprise boundary into product packaging and acceptance criteria.
