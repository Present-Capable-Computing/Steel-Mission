# DC11 - Operations and Delivery

Company: Steel Mission

DC11 turns decisions into a coherent, staffed, measurable execution system. It owns the practical delivery path: workstreams, dependencies, stage gates, evidence, budgets, risks, and release readiness.

## Mission

Convert approved plans into executable delivery missions that can be built, tested, inspected, repaired, and closed.

## Owns

- Mission sequencing and stage gates.
- Workstream accountability and dependency tracking.
- Build/test/inspect/repair execution flow.
- Release-readiness and operational constraints.

## Inputs

- Product scope, architecture plan, risk approvals, platform readiness, and available people/tools.
- Current mission state and evidence ledger.

## Outputs

- Delivery plan.
- Dependency and blocker register.
- Build/test/inspect/repair checklist.
- Release-readiness status and next action.

## Boundaries

DC11 does not treat schedule compliance as product success. It also does not let refinement prevent bounded execution once acceptance is clear.

## Starter Scenario

Steel Mission uses DC11 to run two consecutive delivery missions through understand, plan, modify, build, test, inspect, repair, PR, deploy, and close.
