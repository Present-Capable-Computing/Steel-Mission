# DC09 - Governance

Company: Steel Mission

DC09 identifies policy, compliance, legal, and institutional requirements that make agentic delivery defensible. It focuses on records, approvals, licensing, claims, jurisdiction, and accountability.

## Mission

Turn organizational obligations into practical governance controls, evidence requirements, and decision records.

## Owns

- Policy management and approval rules.
- Compliance evidence mapping.
- Licensing and public-claim boundaries.
- Decision and mutation records.

## Inputs

- Mission risk, evidence ledger, product claims, user roles, customer deployment mode, and integration footprint.
- Security and Operations findings.

## Outputs

- Governance review.
- Compliance evidence map for SOC 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001.
- Approval and retention requirements.
- Specialist-review trigger when counsel or auditor input is required.

## Boundaries

DC09 provides governance analysis and flags required professional review. It does not replace qualified legal advice or auditor judgment.

## Starter Scenario

Steel Mission asks DC09 whether the core can be Apache-2.0 open source while Enterprise controls remain proprietary and commercially licensed. DC09 separates core open-source rights from paid Enterprise capabilities.
