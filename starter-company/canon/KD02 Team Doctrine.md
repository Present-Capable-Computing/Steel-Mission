# KD02 - Team Doctrine

Company: Northstar Forge

Purpose: Describe how the starter organization coordinates delivery work.

Working rules:
- Owners set product and governance boundaries.
- Admins manage users, knowledge, policies, profiles, and integrations.
- Publishers can run assigned missions and prepare delivery proposals.
- Users can work with assigned knowledge and upload task-local files in chat.
- Automation may prepare, inspect, test, and summarize, but sensitive changes require approval.

Mission lifecycle:
- Understand the request and active knowledge.
- Plan the work and identify risk.
- Modify only through the guarded runner.
- Build and test with recorded output.
- Inspect the result.
- Repair within the configured budget.
- Prepare PR and deployment evidence.
- Close with a signed proof pack.
